## 2. Comprehensive Business Entity Catalog (18 Core Models)

### 2.1 Party Master (`party.party`)
- **Technical Model:** `party.party`
- **Purpose:** Represents individual persons, corporate entities, insurers, and clinic staff.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `name` (Char, Required): Full legal name.
  - `is_person` (Boolean): `True` for individual humans.
  - `is_patient` (Boolean): `True` for clinic patients.
  - `gender` (Selection): `'m'` (Male), `'f'` (Female).
  - `dob` (Date): Date of birth.
  - `fed_country` (Many2One `country.country`): Country of origin / nationality (e.g., `QAT`).
  - `ref` (Char, Unique): National identification number (Qatar QID / Civil ID).
- **Relationships:**
  - `addresses` (One2Many `party.address`): Physical contact addresses.
  - `contact_mechanisms` (One2Many `party.contact_mechanism`): Phone, email.

### 2.2 Patient Clinical File (`gnuhealth.patient`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.patient`
- **Purpose:** Outpatient medical record file linking a person to clinical history and medical record number.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `party` (Many2One `party.party`, Required, Unique): Associated identity.
  - `puid` (Char, Unique, Read-Only): Patient Unique Identifier generated by system sequence (e.g., `KQI816APL`).
  - `blood_type` (Selection): `'A'`, `'B'`, `'AB'`, `'O'`.
  - `rh` (Selection): `'+'`, `'-'`.
  - `general_info` (Text): Background clinical notes, baseline alerts.
- **Immutability:** `puid` is strictly system-generated and immutable once created.

### 2.3 Outpatient Appointment (`gnuhealth.appointment`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.appointment`
- **Purpose:** Manages clinic visit scheduling, queue tracking, and provider allocation.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `patient` (Many2One `gnuhealth.patient`, Required): Scheduled patient.
  - `healthprof` (Many2One `gnuhealth.healthprofessional`, Required): Attending physician.
  - `specialty` (Many2One `gnuhealth.specialty`): Medical department.
  - `appointment_date` (DateTime, Required): Scheduled arrival timestamp.
  - `state` (Selection): `'free'` -> `'confirmed'` -> `'checked_in'` -> `'done'` (or `'user_cancelled'`).
- **State Machine Rules:**
  - An appointment cannot transition to `checked_in` without a confirmed patient.
  - Only `checked_in` appointments can be linked to active clinical consultations.

### 2.4 Patient Clinical Evaluation (`gnuhealth.patient.evaluation`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.patient.evaluation`
- **Purpose:** Clinical consultation and nursing triage record.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `patient` (Many2One `gnuhealth.patient`, Required).
  - `healthprof` (Many2One `gnuhealth.healthprofessional`, Required).
  - `appointment` (Many2One `gnuhealth.appointment`).
  - `chief_complaint` (Char, Required).
  - `present_illness` (Text): Subjective history.
  - `evaluation_summary` (Text): Objective examination findings.
  - `info_diagnosis` (Text): Clinical assessment narrative.
  - `directions` (Text): Therapeutic management plan.
  - `diagnosis` (Many2One `gnuhealth.pathology`, Required for sign-off): ICD-10 diagnosis.
  - Vital signs: `systolic`, `diastolic`, `bpm`, `temperature`, `respiratory_rate`, `osat`, `weight`, `height`, `bmi`.
  - `state` (Selection): `'draft'` -> `'in_progress'` -> `'signed'`.
- **Immutability:** Once `state == 'signed'`, the entire record becomes read-only (`states={'readonly': Eval('state') == 'signed'}`). Deletion is denied.

### 2.5 Medical Diagnoses & Pathology (`gnuhealth.pathology`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.pathology`
- **Purpose:** Standardized disease nomenclature (ICD-10 coding).
- **Key Fields:**
  - `code` (Char, Required, Unique): ICD-10 code (e.g., `J06.9`).
  - `name` (Char, Required): Official clinical title (e.g., "Acute upper respiratory infection, unspecified").
  - `category` (Many2One `gnuhealth.pathology.category`): Anatomical/system classification.

### 2.6 Electronic Prescription Order (`gnuhealth.prescription.order`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.prescription.order`
- **Purpose:** Header for outpatient medication orders.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `name` (Char, Read-Only): Prescription number sequence (e.g., `RX014`).
  - `patient` (Many2One `gnuhealth.patient`, Required).
  - `healthprof` (Many2One `gnuhealth.healthprofessional`, Required).
  - `prescription_date` (DateTime, Required).
  - `prescription_warning_ack` (Boolean, Required): Mandatory safety confirmation.
  - `state` (Selection): `'draft'` -> `'done'` (Validated).

### 2.7 Electronic Prescription Line (`gnuhealth.prescription.line`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.prescription.line`
- **Purpose:** Specific pharmaceutical item, formulation, and dosing instructions.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `order` (Many2One `gnuhealth.prescription.order`, Required).
  - `medicament` (Many2One `gnuhealth.medicament`, Required): Catalog product.
  - `dose` (Decimal, Required): Quantitative strength (e.g., `500.0`).
  - `dose_unit` (Many2One `gnuhealth.dose.unit`, Required): e.g., `mg`.
  - `form` (Many2One `gnuhealth.drug.form`, Required): e.g., `Tablet`, `Capsule`.
  - `route` (Many2One `gnuhealth.drug.route`, Required): e.g., `Oral`.
  - `frequency` (Integer, Required): Times per day.
  - `duration` (Integer, Required): Numerical duration.
  - `duration_period` (Selection, Required): `'days'`, `'weeks'`, `'months'`.
  - `qty` (Integer, Required): Total dispensed quantity.

### 2.8 Laboratory Test Requisition (`gnuhealth.lab`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.lab`
- **Purpose:** Diagnostic pathology requisition header.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `name` (Char, Read-Only): Test order code (e.g., `TEST037`).
  - `patient` (Many2One `gnuhealth.patient`, Required).
  - `requestor` (Many2One `gnuhealth.healthprofessional`, Required).
  - `test` (Many2One `gnuhealth.lab.test_type`, Required): e.g., `Complete Blood Count`.
  - `state` (Selection): `'draft'` -> `'tested'` -> `'done'`.

### 2.9 Laboratory Analyte Result (`gnuhealth.lab.test.critearea`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.lab.test.critearea`
- **Purpose:** Individual chemical, biological, or hematological analyte finding.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `gnuhealth_lab_id` (Many2One `gnuhealth.lab`, Required).
  - `name` (Char, Required): Analyte label (e.g., `Hemoglobin (HGB)`).
  - `result` (Char/Decimal): Entered numerical or qualitative finding (e.g., `14.1`).
  - `lower_limit` (Decimal): Reference lower boundary.
  - `upper_limit` (Decimal): Reference upper boundary.
  - `units` (Many2One `gnuhealth.lab.test.unit`): Measurement unit (e.g., `g/dL`).

### 2.10 Medical Imaging Request (`gnuhealth.imaging.test.request`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.imaging.test.request`
- **Purpose:** Diagnostic radiology request.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `order_code` (Char, Read-Only): Order sequence code (e.g., `RAD-00012`).
  - `patient` (Many2One `gnuhealth.patient`, Required).
  - `doctor` (Many2One `gnuhealth.healthprofessional`, Required).
  - `requested_test` (Many2One `gnuhealth.imaging.test`, Required): e.g., `Chest X-Ray`.
  - `state` (Selection): `'draft'` -> `'requested'` -> `'done'`.

### 2.11 Medical Imaging Result (`gnuhealth.imaging.test.result`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.imaging.test.result`
- **Purpose:** Formal narrative report issued by reporting radiologist.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `request` (Many2One `gnuhealth.imaging.test.request`, Required).
  - `comment` (Text, Required): Radiologist diagnostic impressions.
  - `date` (DateTime, Required): Validation timestamp.

### 2.12 Health Service Bundle (`gnuhealth.health_service`)
- **Technical Model:** `gnuhealth.health_service`
- **Purpose:** Encapsulates all billable items generated during an outpatient encounter.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `patient` (Many2One `gnuhealth.patient`, Required).
  - `desc` (Char, Required): Encounter summary.
  - `service_date` (Date, Required).
  - `lines` (One2Many `gnuhealth.health_service.line`): Billable items.

### 2.13 Customer Invoice (`account.invoice`)
- **Technical Model:** `account.invoice`
- **Purpose:** Legal commercial invoice generating accounts receivable entries.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `number` (Char, Read-Only): Invoice number sequence (e.g., `INV-2026/00014`).
  - `party` (Many2One `party.party`, Required): Patient or billing party.
  - `type` (Selection): `'out'` (Customer Invoice).
  - `currency` (Many2One `currency.currency`): Fixed to Qatari Riyal (`QAR`).
  - `total_amount` (Decimal, Read-Only): Calculated sum of lines.
  - `state` (Selection): `'draft'` -> `'validated'` -> `'posted'` -> `'paid'`.
- **Immutability:** Once posted, lines cannot be added, edited, or deleted.

### 2.14 Customer Invoice Line (`account.invoice.line`)
- **Technical Model:** `account.invoice.line`
- **Purpose:** Individual billable service or diagnostic fee line item.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `invoice` (Many2One `account.invoice`, Required).
  - `product` (Many2One `product.product`, Required): Clinical service tariff.
  - `account` (Many2One `account.account`, Required): Operating Revenue account (`4000`).
  - `quantity` (Decimal, Required): Quantity delivered.
  - `unit_price` (Decimal, Required): Approved tariff unit price (e.g., `150.00 QAR`).

### 2.15 General Ledger Accounting Move (`account.move`)
- **Technical Model:** `account.move`
- **Purpose:** Authoritative double-entry bookkeeping journal transaction.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `number` (Char, Read-Only): Journal entry number (e.g., Move `47`, Move `48`).
  - `journal` (Many2One `account.journal`, Required): `'Revenue'` or `'Cash'`.
  - `date` (Date, Required): Transaction date.
  - `origin` (Reference): Originating model pointer (e.g., `account.invoice,14`).
  - `state` (Selection): `'draft'` -> `'posted'`.
- **Balancing Invariant:** Total Debits must equal Total Credits exactly (`sum(lines.debit) == sum(lines.credit)`). Unbalanced moves are rejected by database constraint.

### 2.16 General Ledger Move Line (`account.move.line`)
- **Technical Model:** `account.move.line`
- **Purpose:** Individual debit or credit line item in the general ledger.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `move` (Many2One `account.move`, Required).
  - `account` (Many2One `account.account`, Required): Target GL account (1000 Cash, 1200 AR, 4000 Revenue).
  - `debit` (Decimal): Debit amount in QAR.
  - `credit` (Decimal): Credit amount in QAR.
  - `party` (Many2One `party.party`): Associated customer party for Accounts Receivable tracking.
  - `reconciliation` (Many2One `account.move.reconciliation`): Reconciliation link.

### 2.17 Account Reconciliation (`account.move.reconciliation`)
- **Technical Model:** `account.move.reconciliation`
- **Purpose:** Settles matching debit and credit lines on Accounts Receivable.
- **Key Fields:**
  - `name` (Char, Unique): Reconciliation sequence identifier.
  - `lines` (One2Many `account.move.line`): Linked debit (invoice) and credit (payment) lines.
- **Result:** Reduces customer net outstanding receivable balance to `0.00 QAR`.

### 2.18 Users and Roles (`res.user`, `res.group`)
- **Technical Models:** `res.user`, `res.group`
- **Purpose:** System operator accounts and security role assignments.
- **Key Fields (`res.user`):**
  - `login` (Char, Required, Unique): User login username.
  - `password_hash` (Char, Read-Only): Scrypt/SHA-256 password hash.
  - `groups` (Many2Many `res.group`): Security group memberships.

## 3. Clinical Workflow Lifecycle & State Machine Transitions

```
[ FRONT DESK ]              [ NURSING ]                 [ PHYSICIAN ]
Registration                Triage                      Consultation
party.party (Create)        eval.create (Vitals)        eval.write (SOAP)
     |                           |                           |
     v                           v                           v
appt.create (confirmed)     eval.write (Triage)         eval.end_evaluation (signed)
     |                                                       |
     v                                                       v
appt.write (checked_in)                                 presc.create_prescription (done)
                                                             |
                                                             v
                                                        lab.create / rad.create
                                                             |
                                                             v
                                                  [ BILLING & CASHIER ]
                                                  invoice.create -> invoice.post
                                                             |
                                                             v
                                                  pay_wizard -> invoice.paid
```

### State Machine Transition Rules Matrix

| Model | Initial State | Trigger Action / Method | Next State | Responsible Role | Enforced Constraints |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `gnuhealth.appointment` | `free` | Booking (`write`) | `confirmed` | Front Desk | Patient and Physician required. |
| `gnuhealth.appointment` | `confirmed` | Check-In (`write`) | `checked_in` | Front Desk / Nurse | Patient must be physically present. |
| `gnuhealth.patient.evaluation`| `draft` | Save Vitals (`write`)| `in_progress` | Nurse / Physician | Valid systolic, diastolic, heart rate. |
| `gnuhealth.patient.evaluation`| `in_progress` | `end_evaluation` | `signed` | Physician | Primary ICD-10 diagnosis required. Record locks permanently. |
| `gnuhealth.prescription.order`| `draft` | `create_prescription`| `done` | Physician | Safety warning acknowledged; lines valid. |
| `gnuhealth.lab` | `draft` | `complete_criteareas` | `draft` (loaded) | Lab Tech | Criteria parameters auto-generated. |
| `gnuhealth.lab` | `draft` | `generate_document` | `done` | Lab Tech | Quantitative results entered. |
| `gnuhealth.imaging.test.request`| `draft` | `requested` / `generate` | `done` | Radiologist | Narrative impression recorded. |
| `account.invoice` | `draft` | `post` | `posted` | Cashier | Generates Move 47; locks lines permanently. |
| `account.invoice` | `posted` | `pay` wizard | `paid` | Cashier | Generates Move 48; reconciles AR to 0.00. |

## 4. Accounting & General Ledger Double-Entry Logic

```
1. Customer Invoice Posting (Move #47 - Revenue Journal):
   DEBIT:  Account 1200 - Accounts Receivable (Party: Patient)   150.00 QAR
   CREDIT: Account 4000 - Medical Services Revenue               150.00 QAR
   Balance: 150.00 QAR == 150.00 QAR (Balanced)

2. Cashier Payment Settlement (Move #48 - Cash Journal):
   DEBIT:  Account 1000 - Petty Cash / Main Till                 150.00 QAR
   CREDIT: Account 1200 - Accounts Receivable (Party: Patient)   150.00 QAR
   Balance: 150.00 QAR == 150.00 QAR (Balanced)

3. Reconciliation:
   Line 47.AR matched with Line 48.AR -> Reconciled.
   Net Patient Receivable Balance = 0.00 QAR (Paid in Full).
```
